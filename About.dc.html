About Organic Exports — Coimbatore Vegetable Sourcing & Supply

Organic Exports began with a simple observation: Coimbatore grows some of India’s best vegetables, but too much of it is sold loose, ungraded and at the mercy of the day’s mandi price.

We work directly with grower families across Thondamuthur, Karamadai, Pollachi, Udumalpet and Palladam. Harvests are planned against confirmed buyer orders, so farmers get a dependable outlet and buyers get produce picked for them — not whatever happened to be at market.

Everything passes through our packhouse on Mettupalayam Road, where it is graded, packed and tagged before it leaves on trucks bound for other states.

WHAT WE STAND FOR
Grade honestly A Grade A bag means Grade A to the bottom. We never top-dress lots or mix grades to hit a price.
Pay growers on time Farmers are paid on a fixed weekly cycle. It is why the best growers keep their harvest for us.
Say it before it happens If rain, a price spike or a delayed truck affects your order, you hear from us first — with options.
